RULE 1: BOLA Detection (API1:2023)
----------------------------------------------
index=api_logs "community/posts/recent"
| rex field=_raw "(?<attacker_ip>\d+\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+)"
| stats count by attacker_ip
| where count > 5
| eval severity="HIGH"
| eval attack_type="BOLA - API1:2023"
| table attacker_ip count severity attack_type

RESULT: 370 requests detected, severity HIGH

RULE 3: Rate Limiting Abuse (API4:2023)
----------------------------------------------
index=api_logs "identity/api/v2/user/dashboard"
| rex field=_raw "(?<attacker_ip>\d+\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+)"
| bucket _time span=1m
| stats count as requests_per_min by _time attacker_ip
| where requests_per_min > 10
| eval severity="HIGH"
| eval attack_type="Rate Limiting Abuse - API4:2023"
| table _time attacker_ip requests_per_min severity attack_type

RESULT: 100 requests/min detected, severity HIGH

RULE 4: User Enumeration (API1:2023)
----------------------------------------------
index=api_logs "user/" "404"
| rex field=_raw "(?<attacker_ip>\d+\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+)"
| rex field=_raw "user/(?<user_id>\d+)"
| stats dc(user_id) as unique_ids count by attacker_ip
| where unique_ids > 5
| eval severity="HIGH"
| eval attack_type="User Enumeration - API1:2023"
| table attacker_ip unique_ids count severity attack_type

RESULT: 30 unique IDs probed, 105 requests, severity HIGH
